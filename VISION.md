# MataLens — Product Vision

**Working name:** MataLens (`matalens` on GitHub)  
**Project status:** Concept / build kickoff  
**One-line promise:** **See what matters. Know what to do next.**

## The problem

People encounter public processes and institutions through disconnected websites, confusing official guidance, inaccessible political information, scattered news, and reporting forms that rarely make the next step clear. A citizen shouldn't need to become a policy expert or programmer simply to understand an election, prepare for a civic responsibility, or document a broken public facility.

The goal is **not another election news site or another AI chatbot**. It is a consumer-grade **personal civic companion** with a guided, satisfying path from knowledge → preparation → action → traceable evidence.

## The actual vision

MataLens begins with election preparation because it is a timely, concrete entry point, but is **not limited to politics**. Over time, it can help people understand public rights and processes, contribute verified public evidence, report community issues and follow up on their own civic actions.

- For elections: understand rights and the significance of voting/PVCs; find one's official polling unit; learn which offices are contested and when; review all relevant candidates and running mates; explore sourced candidate histories, policies and relevant records; privately save intended choices on-device; practise recognizing names, faces, party symbols and official procedures; prepare reminders and directions; stay informed about verified updates.
- For election evidence: photograph **publicly posted polling-unit result sheets** where lawful and safe; label the submission with the relevant polling unit; preserve originals for later lawful processing/collaboration. **We are not building automated extraction, vote collation, or a competing official results service as the central innovation.**
- For ongoing civic life: photograph a problematic road, drain, public facility, suspected unsafe product or another verifiable community issue; explain it in one's own words or voice; optionally use AI to organize a **neutral, editable, factual description**; add a location with consent; submit a documented report; receive a receipt and honest updates. Actual agency routing comes only after confirmed integrations or a verifiable manual process.
- For the long term: create a user-owned place for civic preparation, reports and follow-ups. Make it possible for relevant external civic/open-source projects to receive evidence **only through consented, reviewed and compatible integration**.

## What already exists

The founder likes **VotersDesk** as a reference for excellent civic information experiences. Others have built candidate directories, vote quizzes, offline plans, promise tracking, polling-unit discovery, voter education and local issue reporting. **None of these individual features should be claimed as invented by MataLens.**

Similarly, **FixMyStreet** is a major inspiration for location-based citizen reporting, photo handling, issue routing and case status; **Ushahidi** for community evidence intake. Use research and compatible concepts to build a well-integrated, easier, more polished experience; establish differentiation through honest usability testing.

## Founder priorities

- **Ease of use is as important as functionality.** A first-time user should navigate without being coached.
- Visually **premium, modern, green-and-white, subtly glossy**; dark green rather than bland gray or neon-black sci-fi.
- Brand is **not AI-ey**: no default chatbot gimmick, giant neural orbs, fake analytics, or generic admin-template looks.
- Deliver a **working** photo submission and traceable user experience, rather than flashy simulated government acknowledgements.
- The product is **broader than politics**, but do not bloat the initial version with unfinished domains.
- It is a **side project for the AIESEC hackathon**; protect the founder's time for MY-AI. Let Codex own conventional web-app implementation where possible.
- Build to a global-quality product bar. Nigerian electoral content is the initial source-domain/context, **not a permanent geographic business limitation**.

## Ethical and security boundaries

The platform is **nonpartisan**. It presents accurately sourced information and neutral learning tools, not candidate endorsements, election-outcome predictions, persuasion targeting, hidden preference profiling or fabricated political claims.

A user's personally selected candidates are **private local preparation notes**, never voter instructions and never transmitted to servers. Do not request or store PVC numbers/images or infer people’s political affiliations.

Election-result submissions concern **publicly displayed polling-unit result sheets**, never a marked ballot or anything exposing a secret vote. Do not claim affiliation with INEC or any government agency; preserve provenance and label unofficial user submissions as such.

Citizen reports can be inaccurate or harmful. Build privacy controls, moderation and careful status wording. Do not publicly accuse specific individuals or identify medicines as counterfeit using AI. Explain that claims require verification.

## Success definition

A visitor should be able to understand the product in seconds and complete a real end-to-end journey:
1. Learn one important civic fact with clear source links.
2. Choose a relevant electoral area, understand the offices/candidates, and prepare offline-ready reminders and practice.
3. Submit an appropriately safe test public-evidence photograph or public-infrastructure report, then receive and revisit a real platform receipt.
4. Never be misled about what MataLens has and has not verified, submitted or accomplished.

**The vision is ambitious; the implementation must be honest.**
