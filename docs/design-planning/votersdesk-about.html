About VotersDesk

Know who is asking for your vote

VotersDesk is an independent, non-partisan guide to Nigeria's elections. Our aim is simple: every Nigerian voter should be able to find, in one trustworthy place, who is running on their ballot, what they stand for, and where that information comes from.

Why we exist

Information about candidates is scattered across INEC documents, party websites, social media and news reports. It is hard to find, harder to compare, and often impossible to verify, especially for Senate, House and State Assembly races, where most voters cannot name who is running.

We bring that record together, label where every fact comes from, and treat every candidate and party the same way. Then we keep following through: after the election, we track what winners promised against what they deliver.

Our ground rules

  1. 01

    Every candidate is treated the same

    Same layout, same fields, same sourcing standard. Lists are alphabetical unless you choose to sort them. Party colours appear only inside a party's own badge, never as page decoration.

  2. 02

    Evidence before claims

    Every fact links to where it came from. Official records, party documents and reporting are labelled separately, so you can judge the source yourself.

  3. 03

    We do not endorse

    We never tell you who to vote for. Quiz results, comparisons and user polls are tools for reading the record, not advice.

  4. 04

    Mistakes are corrected in public

    Anyone can report an error on any profile. Confirmed fixes are listed on the corrections page with the date.

  5. 05

    Your choices stay yours

    Quiz answers and your voting plan stay on your phone and are never sent to us.

Where our information comes from

Who is a candidate, for which party and constituency
INEC's published final lists of candidates. These are the source of truth; we update when INEC does.
Candidate particulars
Forms INEC publishes for public scrutiny. We use election-relevant facts only and never republish home addresses or other private details.
Manifestos and positions
Parties' and candidates' own published documents, and their answers to our questionnaire.
News and context
Established Nigerian and international media, always linked and dated.

What we are not

VotersDesk is not part of INEC, any government body or any political party. For voter registration, polling units, election dates and official results, INEC is always the final authority.

Our user poll reflects VotersDesk users who choose to answer, not the Nigerian public.
