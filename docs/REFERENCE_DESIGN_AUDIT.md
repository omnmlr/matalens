# MataLens reference design audit

19 November 2026 (WAT) · Implementation baseline `558c9ff` · Research and specification

Give first-time visitors a direct route to a sourced ballot, a polling-unit lookup and a private plan. Make Civic Camera a separate path with photo safety before capture and a receipt immediately after successful submission. Keep the existing guest access, source records and receipt-backed reporting while reorganizing what the visitor sees first.

## FixMyStreet · Camera and reporting

1. **Choose the entry that matches what the reporter has.** The UK homepage presents postcode/street entry, current-location lookup and photo-first entry. The upstream photo-first tests specify that an image carrying GPS goes to a pin check, whereas a photo without it stays attached while the reporter supplies a location. The useful pattern is preserving the photo while resolving missing context. [UK homepage](https://www.fixmystreet.com/) · [Photo-first test definitions](https://github.com/mysociety/fixmystreet/blob/3f38dcc104a7cc94303e62b1d77fd9e07ff598fa/.cypress/cypress/e2e/photo_first.cy.js)
2. **Confirm the place once.** The upstream mobile code makes the map a full-screen task with crosshairs and a confirmation action. Its tests at 480 px expect that action to lead straight to category selection. The 1200 px cases expect a separate photo confirmation with preview. Both routes lead to category, attachment and problem details, with the existing photo retained. MataLens can use the same principle with a short descriptive location field and a locally held preview. [Mobile controller](https://github.com/mysociety/fixmystreet/blob/3f38dcc104a7cc94303e62b1d77fd9e07ff598fa/web/cobrands/fixmystreet/fixmystreet.js#L254-L298) · [Report steps](https://github.com/mysociety/fixmystreet/blob/3f38dcc104a7cc94303e62b1d77fd9e07ff598fa/templates/web/base/report/new/form_report.html)
3. **Distinguish a draft, an uploaded attachment and a confirmed report.** The manual describes offline drafts that require manual resumption and submission. It also explains that guests can confirm by email and that confirmed reports can be published on the map and routed to an authority. MataLens should use a private receipt for recovery and display its actual moderation status. [Citizen workflow and offline reporting](https://fixmystreet.org/pro-manual/citizens-experience/) · [Routing and public reports](https://fixmystreet.org/how-it-works/)

## Original redesign specification

### 1. Give Home and My Election different jobs

- **Home (`/`)** is a compact launch screen. Use a practical heading such as **Get ready to vote**. Put **See candidates** first and **Find my polling unit** beside or immediately below it, with a prominent **Document an issue** link to Civic Camera. Put the next dated, verified event in a plain text band with its official source and scope. Show **Continue my plan** only after there is saved progress. **Remove the original generated illustration entirely**, not merely relocate it. Do not substitute stock-style or synthetic hero imagery: use original typography and useful sourced civic data. Photographs belong only where directly informative (authentic, rights-cleared candidate portraits or user-submitted evidence).
- **My Election (`/election`)** starts with **Candidates**, **Polling unit** and **My plan** as stable anchor links. Move the candidate list ahead of the multi-field plan on phones. Group source-linked election dates, checklist, calendar export and practice under preparation. Keep the existing `/practice` route directly linked from Home or My Election. Use four persistent navigation entries on mobile and desktop: **Home**, **My Election**, **Civic Camera** and **My Actions**. Keep Sources & updates and Privacy available through the footer and relevant screens.
- **Polling-unit handoff** identifies INEC and explains what to bring back, such as a public polling-unit name or code. A guest can add it to the private plan or skip it and still browse the ballot. When offline, keep previously cached guides and local plan available and explain that official lookup requires a connection.

### 2. Make the ballot easy to scan and verify

- At `/election#candidates`, show **Surulere I**, **Lagos**, **House of Representatives** and **8 candidates on the INEC list** before the first result. Show the original list and its review date alongside this heading. Label the one-constituency coverage clearly. For an unsupported area, provide the official list handoff and a direct way back to the example. A state entered in a private plan supplies context only.
- Use a single-column roster with consistent rows. Show full name first, party abbreviation and name as secondary text, and separate **View details** and **Save privately** actions. The default list is alphabetical by displayed name with a visible count. Search trims whitespace and matches name, party abbreviation or full party name. Put **Clear search** in the empty state and announce the changed result count without moving focus.
- Open details in a keyboard-operable disclosure that preserves the search, scroll position and focus when closed. Start with office, constituency and party, then the sourced age and listed qualifications. Link the INEC list beside the facts. Add biography, history or positions only when sourced and dated. Use a text-first presentation for everyone and leave a same-race comparison out of this release until there are richer comparable records. Keep bookmarks and personal notes in the existing browser-local plan.

### 3. Separate capture from submission

| Stage | Required UI behavior |
| --- | --- |
| **Choose a report type** | Offer **Community issue** and **Public result sheet**. Before the picker, show guidance for the type. A result-sheet report requires a publicly displayed sheet, safe capture and no marked ballot. For a community issue, ask for the problem and public surroundings without identifiable people or private details. |
| **Add a photo and context** | Offer camera-capable selection and an existing-file picker where supported. Show a local preview with Replace and Remove and keep the selected file when editing context. Accept the current server formats (still JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB and 25 megapixels). Require polling-unit name/code for a result sheet. A community issue needs a category and factual description, with optional public location. Explain the original may contain camera metadata. Do not infer location from that metadata. |
| **Review and submit** | Show the selected image, type, description, location as entered and the privacy/retention terms together. **Submit to MataLens** is the sole sending action. Prevent duplicate taps while it is pending. If offline or intake is disabled, show the specific reason early. Preserve the current draft in the open tab through a recoverable failure. Reuse the pending receipt on an uncertain retry and allow My Actions to check whether the upload completed. |
| **Confirm and recover** | After server acknowledgement, show **Submitted to MataLens**, actual status, and **Download receipt** as the primary next action. Explain who can use the receipt, lost-code limits and the 30-day retention period. **My Actions** separates election preparation from Civic Camera receipts and supports recovery from a copied code or link in a fresh browser. Keep receipt previews private and require explicit confirmation before withdrawal. |

Use the existing intake/receipt API, upload validators, idempotent receipt handling and review/withdrawal states. Defer persistent photo drafts, maps, public publishing and authority routing. [Current Camera and My Actions](../src/camera.tsx) · [Upload contract](../server/upload.mjs) · [Existing verification](IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md)

### 4. Set the visual and release bar

- Use MataLens’s own forest-green and warm-white surfaces, readable dark forest mode, restrained surface highlights and a single strong action color. Start with 16 px mobile gutters, 16 px body text, 44 px touch targets and a clear focus ring. Use the editorial face for the main heading and the existing legible sans for labels, candidate facts and forms. On wide screens, keep the main roster or form in reading order and add a quiet supporting rail for official links or review details. At 320 px, use one content column and keep persistent navigation clear of content, focused controls and the phone safe area. Use text and icons alongside status color, and respect reduced motion.
- **First release check.** From a fresh 320 px guest viewport, reach both the candidate roster and the official polling-unit handoff in one action from Home. In an unprompted 60-second walkthrough, ask a first-time visitor to find the current race’s source and explain what their area’s coverage is. Confirm keyboard navigation, 200% zoom, focus return after details, search zero/reset and usable layouts at 320, 390, 768 and 1280 px in light and dark themes. Capture these as MataLens screenshots for review.
- **Report release check.** Use a synthetic image in the local environment for both report types. Check invalid photo, denied camera permission with file-picker recovery, offline and disabled intake, loss of the submission response, single-report retry, fresh-browser receipt recovery and withdrawal. Verify the file/description survive a recoverable failure in the open tab and the report status is clear on reconnect. Inspect controls on a physical Android device where available. The existing test suite provides guest flow and receipt coverage. [Browser tests](../tests/guest.spec.mjs)
- **Implementation order.** First deliver the home links, election hierarchy and sourced roster. Then reshape Civic Camera and My Actions around the existing submission contract. Finally verify the two journeys and capture responsive screenshots. Use the [design direction](../DESIGN.md) and [product constraints](../PRODUCT.md) to resolve details during implementation.
