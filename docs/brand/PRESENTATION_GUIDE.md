# MataLens presentation guide

## 16:9 deck setup

Set the canvas to 1920 × 1080 px or PowerPoint Widescreen, 13.33 × 7.5 in. Use roughly 88–96 px side margins, 72–80 px vertical margins, a 12-column grid and 24 px gutters. Use warm ivory `#F8F8F0` as the default. Use deep forest `#0B1D16` for the opening, a section break and the close. Use the [design system](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) for the complete light and dark palette.

| Role | Family | Suggested PowerPoint size | Treatment |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Title / opening statement | Newsreader Medium 500 | 56–64 pt | 0.98–1.08 line height, −0.03 em if supported |
| Standard slide title | Newsreader Medium 500 | 40–48 pt | 1.04–1.12, −0.02 em |
| Section divider | Newsreader Medium 500 | 52–60 pt | One clear thought, optional italic phrase |
| Subtitle | Manrope 500–600 | 22–28 pt | 1.25–1.35, normal tracking |
| Body | Manrope 400–500 | 18–22 pt | 1.35–1.5, normal tracking |
| Stat | Manrope 750–800 | 38–48 pt | Include units and denominator close by |
| Label / caption / footer | Manrope 650–750 | 12–15 pt | 1.2–1.4, small uppercase labels at +0.08 em |

Use Newsreader and Manrope desktop fonts where the presentation tool supports them. If a substitute is necessary, use Georgia and Aptos or Segoe UI. Export PDF and review the projected version to confirm fonts and line breaks.

## Layout recipes

- **Opening.** Deep forest, small logo lockup, one 56–64 pt line, one sentence below. Keep the mark clear of the headline.
- **Standard.** Warm ivory, short title, a 7/5 or 6/6 split. Set one thesis on the left and evidence or a screenshot on the right.
- **Product.** One desktop screenshot, or two mobile screens that show a before-and-after interaction. Use a 1 px rule and 8–12 px corners. Crop to the interaction and label what changed. Keep screenshots unwarped and cover private receipts.
- **Evidence or architecture.** Use aligned soft-white panels with thin lines and two or three arrows. Label browser-local preferences, the reviewed API, and the actual storage boundary. Add a short source label under evidence. Use one green for the highlighted series and a muted neutral for context.
- **Callout.** Use one tinted panel, a short sentence and a verified citation. Keep margins 24–32 px inside and use ink text.
- **Footer.** Keep a hairline above a Manrope 12–13 pt line. Use “MataLens” at left, a short source or context label in the centre where needed, and the slide number at right. Use `#D6DFD3` on ivory or `#344E40` on dark. Preserve generous space above the footer.

## Recommended pitch sequence

| Slide | Visual structure | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | Dark statement | MataLens and the brand promise |
| Problem | Split with three short points | Fragmented official sources, difficult comparison, and uncertainty about next steps |
| Solution | Four-step flow | Discover → Read → Check sources → Practise |
| Product | Annotated screen pair | Follow a politician, open a reviewed story, inspect its evidence |
| Architecture | Three small boxes | Browser and local preferences → reviewed same-origin API; separately label the actual Camera storage mode |
| Innovation | Evidence panel close-up | Date and publisher, original links, distinction between claims and records, sourced quiz |
| Impact | One factual measure or a task outcome | Use measured results with a denominator or describe what a guest can complete |
| Roadmap | Three simple milestones | MVP, validation, then verified operational expansion |
| Team | Simple two-column list | Names and actual responsibilities |
| Closing | Dark, logo and one action | Website and invitation to try a story. Show a QR code only after checking its URL. |

Use the same serif headline and sans-serif label system throughout. Avoid slide-long paragraphs, fake engagement metrics, audience estimates without evidence and generic illustrations. Identify the actual public intake status in the demo. [Reusable assets](BRAND_ASSETS.md)
