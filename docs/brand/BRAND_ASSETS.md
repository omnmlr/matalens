The header lockup is the aperture tile followed by “MataLens” in Manrope. Recreate it with Mata at weight 850 and Lens at 630, close tracking around −0.075 em, and a gap of approximately 0.3 tile widths. The application header uses a 36 px tile with a 22 px aperture. Use a minimum 24 px tile on screen and around 40 px in a deck. Keep clear space at least half the tile width on every side. Keep the tile green in either theme, with an ink wordmark on light backgrounds or `#EEF4E9` on dark. Use the icon alone for a compact badge.

The app tile uses `#2A7155` to `#12402E` with a `#E6F5DE` aperture. Preserve its scale, colour and rounded rectangle. Keep the browser eye icon and header aperture in their existing roles. Reuse the SVGs without distortion. For a monochrome reproduction, give the aperture one solid foreground on a contrasting rounded tile.

The aperture geometry is from Lucide, ISC license. The deck diagram uses the brand palette and system text roles. It has no external assets. Use original or explicitly licensed imagery for any additional slide illustrations.
