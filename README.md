# MataLens

**See what matters. Know what to do next.**

MataLens is a premium, mobile-first civic utility for understanding public life, preparing for elections, documenting issues that matter, and tracking the actions you take.

**Status:** New side-project repository; not a deployed or production-ready application. This is the AIESEC Innovation Hackathon concept, **not** Francis's primary AI-native hardware/OS venture (MY-AI) and **not** AGUS.

## Product at a glance

- **My Election:** Understand civic rights and election procedures; locate/confirm your polling unit using official sources; see upcoming positions and their candidates; explore neutral, sourced profiles and policies; practise names, ballot symbols and election-day procedures; save private preparation choices **only on the device**; set reminders; receive sourced updates.
- **Civic Camera:** Contribute photographs of **publicly posted result sheets** to a public-evidence intake (without counting or OCR), and document community issues through a reusable evidence-upload workflow.
- **My Actions:** Follow personal preparation progress, saved receipts and the real status of reports. Government submission/routing must not be claimed until technically established.
- **After elections:** Expand to a safe, independently verifiable public-issue reporting network (roads, drainage, public facilities, safety issues, etc.) without becoming solely an elections app.

## Immediate access

MataLens is **guest-first**: no account, login, email, phone number or OTP required for learning, election preparation, practice or civic reports. Personalized voting preparations stay locally on the user's device. Guest uploads should produce securely retrievable case receipts.

REA research is authorized and documented in [docs/REA_RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md](docs/REA_RESEARCH_PLAYBOOK.md). Its earlier working setup was specifically confirmed in Antigravity; current Codex access must be probed rather than assumed.

## Product principles

1. **Extremely easy to understand and use.** Each screen should make the next action obvious.
2. **Premium visual quality:** white/ivory and rich green in light mode, deep forest green in dark mode, restrained gloss, beautifully precise typography, motion and touch feedback—not a generic AI-themed dashboard.
3. **Trust and neutrality:** sourced public information; no partisan persuasion, fabricated government statuses, unsupported accuracy claims or unofficial results presented as certified.
4. **Privacy:** no PVC scans or political preference database; personal candidate choices stay locally on the device; location/photo uploads are opt-in and reviewed for safety.
5. **Build something real:** an end-to-end working app and image uploads, not simulated public service integrations.
6. **Reuse responsibly:** learn from VotersDesk, FixMyStreet, Ushahidi and others, but respect licences and branding. Do not silently incorporate AGPL material.
